The short version

  • Rafe has no account server. Your meals, water, targets and life-stage details are stored on your iPhone.
  • If you sign in with Apple, Rafe syncs that data through your own private iCloud Drive. You can pause it any time.
  • When you ask for a meal estimate, and only after you allow it, that meal’s description or photo is sent to Google’s Gemini AI service. Nothing else about you is sent.
  • Barcode lookups send only the barcode number to public food databases.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and we never sell or share your personal information.
  • You can switch AI estimates off, pause sync, or delete your data inside the app.
  • If you join the Android waitlist on this website, we store your email and an approximate country (from your timezone) with Baserow, only to tell you when Rafe comes to Android. Write to us to be removed.

Mustafa Nabil is responsible for the personal data described in this policy (the data controller). For anything about your privacy, write to mustafanabilinvesting@gmail.com.

On your device, and in your iCloud

Rafe stores your data on your iPhone using Apple’s SwiftData. There is no Rafe account and no Rafe server for the app itself. “No account? No problem. Local is a first-class home.” The Android waitlist on this website is a separate, optional form, described under Android waitlist on this website.

iCloud sync

If you sign in with Apple and iCloud Drive is available, Rafe saves a copy of your data in the app’s private folder in your own iCloud Drive, one small file per device, so a new iPhone can pick up where you left off. Apple stores it under your Apple Account; we cannot see it. What syncs: your profile and settings, meals (text and numbers, not photos), targets, water, reminders, weight history, period dates you logged in Rafe, and your cycle, pregnancy and nursing settings.

To pause sync, open You → Account and turn off iCloud Sync. Pausing keeps your iCloud copy; deleting it is described in Your choices and rights.

Apple Health

If you allow it, Rafe reads your period dates from Apple Health so you don’t enter them twice, and writes the period start dates you log in Rafe back to Health. Dates read from Health are used only in your cycle card on your device and are never uploaded to iCloud or any server. Rafe reads and writes nothing else in Health.

Reminders

Meal and life-stage reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to a server.

AI meal analysis

When you describe or photograph a meal and ask Rafe to estimate it, Rafe first tries to match familiar foods on your iPhone. If it needs the AI, it sends only Rafe’s instruction text plus that meal’s description, or that one photo (JPEG), to the Google Gemini API (generativelanguage.googleapis.com). Your name, age, weight, life stage, cycle or health details are not sent.

  • Why: only to return a calorie and nutrient estimate, which you can edit before saving.
  • When: only after you tap Allow on the “Before Rafe estimates your meal” sheet. Until then, Rafe uses on-device matches only.
  • Your control: switch AI meal estimates (Google Gemini) off any time in the You tab, or simply don’t use photo or text analysis. Manual entry and barcode scanning still work.
  • Retention: Google processes and keeps API data under its own terms. See the Gemini API Additional Terms and Google Privacy Policy.

Rafe never claims that nothing leaves your device: the meal you choose to estimate does.

Third parties

  • Google (Gemini API): receives a meal description or photo for estimates, as above.
  • Open Food Facts and, when configured, USDA FoodData Central: receive only the barcode number you scan, to look up the product. Open Food Facts privacy.
  • RevenueCat: manages subscriptions. It receives an anonymous app user ID, purchase receipts and basic device information. RevenueCat privacy.
  • Apple: App Store purchases, Sign in with Apple, iCloud and Apple Health, under Apple’s privacy policy.
  • Baserow: stores Android waitlist sign-ups from this website (your email and an approximate country). See Baserow’s privacy policy and Android waitlist on this website.

Rafe contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. This website uses no cookies, analytics or third-party scripts, and loads its fonts from its own server. If you join the Android waitlist, this site sends only the details in that form to Baserow; no Baserow script is loaded. Like any web host, the service that serves this website may keep standard request logs (such as IP address, browser type and the page requested) to deliver and protect the site; we do not use them to identify you.

Android waitlist on this website

The Rafe website has an optional waitlist if you want to be told when Rafe comes to Android. It is not part of the iPhone app, and you do not have to join it to use Rafe.

When you submit the form, we store your email address and an approximate country, inferred from your device timezone (not from your IP address), with Baserow. We use this only to announce the Android launch. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or add it to any other list.

To be removed, or to correct the details, write to mustafanabilinvesting@gmail.com and we will delete your sign-up.

Sensitive health information

Cycle, pregnancy and nursing details are sensitive health information. They stay on your device and in your own iCloud. They are not sent to the AI service, not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with employers, insurers, data brokers or anyone else. Rafe does not predict ovulation or fertility.

Your choices and rights

In the app

  • Correct: edit your profile, goals, stage details and any meal in the app.
  • Delete: in You → Danger area choose Delete life-stage data, Delete all local data (keeps your iCloud copy), or Delete account & iCloud data (removes your iCloud copy and signs you out). Deleting the app also removes its on-device data.
  • Export: Rafe Pro includes a PDF meal report and a JSON export of your profile and weight history.
  • Withdraw consent: switch off AI meal estimates, Apple Health access (iOS Settings → Health → Data Access), notifications or iCloud sync at any time.

By email

App data lives on your device and in your iCloud, so we hold no copy of it to look up, export or erase. If you joined the Android waitlist on this website, write to mustafanabilinvesting@gmail.com and we will delete your email and country from that list. If you have a question or a request about data held by a provider listed above, write to the same address and we will help you reach them.

Where you live

If you are in the EU, EEA or UK (GDPR / UK GDPR), you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, to data portability, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data protection authority. The lawful bases are your consent (AI estimates, Apple Health) and performing the service you asked for. If you are in California (CCPA/CPRA), you have the right to know, delete and correct, and to opt out of sale or sharing; we do not sell or share personal information, and we use sensitive personal information only to provide the app.

Children

Rafe is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. Onboarding is designed for ages 14 and up; if you are under the age of majority where you live, please use Rafe with a parent or guardian.

Security and transfers

Rafe talks to services over HTTPS only. Service keys are stored in the iOS Keychain, never in the app’s code. Your on-device data is protected by iOS data protection, and iCloud data by your Apple Account. Google, RevenueCat, Open Food Facts, USDA and Baserow may process data in the United States, the European Union or other countries under their own safeguards.

Changes

If Rafe starts handling data differently, this page and the app’s privacy labels will change in the same update, with a new “last updated” date. Material changes will be shown in the app’s What’s New.
