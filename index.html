How Rafe began.

I built Rafe for one person: my wife. That is the whole reason it exists.

She was nursing, and planning her diet. Every calorie app we tried felt cold, harsh, and shouty. One fixed number, applied the same way every day. Shame when you go over. There was no room in those apps for a body that changes with a cycle, with pregnancy, or with breastfeeding.

So I made something gentle — not another loud calorie app, but a quiet place to plan her meals. I am a solo developer in Gaza. In Rafe, the targets follow her body through each of those seasons: her cycle, pregnancy, and breastfeeding. There are no streaks. Every meal she logs grows a flower.

I built this for her. I named the app Rafe, after her.

— Mustafa, developer of Rafe

Asked, answered.

Is Rafe medical advice?

No. Rafe gives educational nutrition estimates. A target from your clinician always takes precedence.

What’s free, and what does Rafe Pro add?

Free: five AI meal analyses a week, water, your meadow, life-stage cards and ten saved meals. Rafe Pro (weekly, monthly or yearly) adds unlimited analyses, widgets, every palette, a personal meal-report PDF and your full meadow history.

Where is my data?

On your iPhone, and in your private iCloud Drive if you sign in with Apple. Meal estimates use Google’s Gemini AI service, only after you allow it. See the privacy policy.

Which devices and languages?

iPhone with iOS 26 or later. The app is in English; describe meals in any language.
