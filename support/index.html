Help topics

Quick answers, written from how the app works today.

Getting started

Choose your season (cycle, pregnancy, nursing or everyday) and add age, height, weight, activity and goal. Sign in with Apple is optional. Then tap + and describe your first meal.

How is my daily target calculated?

Tap your daily target on Home, then How your target is calculated. Rafe uses resting energy (Mifflin-St Jeor for women) × your activity level, adjusts for your goal, then adds what your stage needs: +340 kcal in the second trimester, +450 in the third, about +330 kcal when nursing. Weight loss is paused in pregnancy, there’s no deficit in the first eight weeks of nursing, and the target never falls below a supportive minimum. A clinician’s target, if you add one, always wins.

Changing your profile, goal or activity

Open the You tab. Your numbers, Activity and Goal rows update your target straight away. Weight check-ins live on the Lightness Path, your weight line.

Life-stage modes

Change stage from the life-stage card on Home. Cycle shows your cycle day and phase once you log a period start (cycle length 21–45 days). Pregnancy shows your week from your due date. Nursing shows your baby’s age from their birth date. Near your due date Rafe asks “Has your baby arrived?” and can switch you to nursing. Your logs stay.

Photo, text and barcode estimates aren’t working
  • Camera: allow camera access in iOS Settings → Rafe → Camera.
  • AI estimates: make sure AI meal estimates (Google Gemini) is on in the You tab. Without it, Rafe uses on-device matches only.
  • Photos: good light, the whole plate in frame, one plate per photo. You can always edit the result.
  • Offline: foods Rafe already knows still match on your iPhone; AI estimates and barcode lookups need an internet connection.
  • Limits: the free plan includes five AI analyses a week and a few camera and barcode tries; the count resets each week.
  • Barcode not found: some products aren’t in public food databases yet. Describe the food instead, or add it manually.
Finding and relogging a past meal

On Home, pull the page down and release to open Your Log History. Choose Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner or Snack (Rafe starts on the one that fits the time of day), search by name (small typos are fine), then tap + next to a plate to log it into that meal again.

Adjusting the portion you ate

After an estimate, What’s inside lists each item with its calories, and everything is editable. Under How much did you actually eat?, slide from ¼ to 2 servings in quarter steps; calories update as you slide. Tap Save to pantry to keep the plate for next time (ten saved meals free, unlimited with Rafe Pro).

Water

Tap +250 or +500, or pour a custom amount. Made a mistake? Tap Undo. Tap the goal to change your daily water goal (from 250 ml up to 8 litres).

Adding the widgets (Rafe Pro)
  1. Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen until the apps jiggle.
  2. Tap Edit → Add Widget, search for Rafe.
  3. Choose small, medium or large and tap Add Widget.

For the Lock Screen: touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize → Lock Screen, then add Rafe. Widgets come with Rafe Pro; without it they show a locked face.

iCloud sync and restoring your data

Sync needs Sign in with Apple and iCloud Drive turned on for your Apple Account. Check You → Account → iCloud Sync. On a new iPhone, sign in to Rafe with the same Apple Account and your data returns from your private iCloud Drive. Pausing sync keeps the iCloud copy. Meal photos stay on the iPhone that took them.

Appearance, themes and app icons

Open You → Appearance to choose light, dark or system, and a theme. Meadow Linen is free. Other palettes unlock as you log meals and climb the Levels (Pearl & Peony at Level 4, Ivory & Onyx at Level 5, Velvet & Verdigris at Level 7), with Rafe Pro, or as a one-time purchase; each locked card shows how many meals are left to earn it free. Use Restore purchases there if a bought theme is missing. Change the app icon in You → App icon.

Notifications and reminders

Set gentle meal reminders in You → Meal reminders. If Rafe says notifications are off, allow them in iOS Settings → Rafe → Notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone; nothing is sent to a server.

Subscriptions: manage, cancel, restore

Rafe Pro (shown as Rafe Premium in the You tab) renews automatically until you cancel. To manage or cancel: iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Rafe. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid the next charge. Switched phones? Open the paywall and tap Restore purchases. For refunds, Apple handles requests at reportaproblem.apple.com. More from Apple: If you want to cancel a subscription.

Deleting your data or account

Open You → Danger area:

  • Delete life-stage data keeps meals, weight and profile.
  • Delete all local data clears this iPhone and keeps your iCloud copy.
  • Delete account & iCloud data removes your iCloud copy and signs you out.

These can’t be undone. Rafe has no server account to close. See the privacy policy.

Report a bug

  1. Note what you tapped, and what you saw.
  2. Take a screenshot or a short screen recording.
  3. Find your Rafe version in iOS Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Rafe.
  4. Send it to mustafanabilinvesting@gmail.com.
